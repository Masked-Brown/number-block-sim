Equal neighbours merge on lock: a pair doubles, a triple 4×, a quad 8×, a quint 16×. Each merge scores its result times the chain index. The floor rises as your top tile grows; retired tiers stop spawning but stay on the board. Overfill a column and fail to merge back down, and the game ends.
